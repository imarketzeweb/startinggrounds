# Project Management: Acquisition to Distribution

The partner's experience of the flip **is** the reporting. The property is managed on site by Charles and the lead contractor; the partner is managed by the Friday Project Update, milestone texts, and one phone call for anything material. Nothing surprises a partner in writing that they did not first hear by phone.

## 1. Project Tracker (Google Sheet, one per deal, in `06-Project-Tracker`)

Tabs:

| Tab | Columns |
|---|---|
| **Budget** | Category, Line item, Contractor, Bid, Approved budget, Spent to date, Committed, Variance, Notes |
| **Schedule** | Phase, Planned start, Planned end, Actual start, Actual end, Status (On track / At risk / Late), Dependency |
| **Draws** | Draw #, Date requested, Amount, For, Approved by, Paid date, Lien waiver received |
| **Issues** | Date, Issue, Impact ($ / days), Decision needed by, Decision, Decided by, Date |
| **Photos** | Date, Phase, Drive link |
| **Vendors** | Trade, Company, Contact, License #, Insurance expiry, W-9 on file |

Z-15 reads Budget and Schedule every Friday for the update.

## 2. Contractor and vendor rules

1. Three bids for any scope over $25k; two for anything over $5k. Same written scope to each bidder.
2. Licensed and insured, certificates on file before day one. Lien waivers with every draw.
3. Written contract with: scope, price, schedule with milestones, draw schedule tied to milestones (never more than 10% up front on the first project with a contractor), change-order process, daily clean-up, penalty/bonus if used.
4. Charles walks the site **weekly, same day and time**, photographs everything, and updates Issues before the Friday update.
5. Change orders over $1,000 or 5 days: written, partner informed the same day, Charles decides (Structure A: partner decides on Charles's recommendation).

## 3. Phase checklist with partner touches

| Phase | Charles / VA actions | Partner receives |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-close** | Contractor contract signed, permits pre-checked, insurance bound (vacant / builder's risk), utilities transfer scheduled, lockbox and cameras planned, materials lead times ordered | Milestone texts (`SP-Buyer Under Contract` offsets), closing wire confirmation call |
| **Week 1: demo and discovery** | Demo, camera the sewer, open walls where scope expects surprises, lock the final scope, update Issues | Friday update #1 with "discovery" section |
| **Rough-ins** | Electrical, plumbing, HVAC, framing; inspections scheduled and passed | Milestone text "rough-ins passed" |
| **Drywall and paint** | Drywall, texture, prime, paint; exterior work in parallel | Milestone photo text |
| **Finishes** | Flooring, kitchen, baths, fixtures, trim, doors, hardware | Milestone photo text; resale plan email 2 weeks before completion |
| **Punch and staging** | Punch list walked with contractor and VA, cleaning, staging, photography booked | "Photos scheduled [date], list date [date]" text |
| **Resale** | Full `05-SELLER-SYSTEM` launch | Monday seller reports every week |
| **Closing and distribution** | Final numbers, distribution statement, CPA/attorney review as needed | Distribution statement within 5 business days, call |

## 4. The Friday Project Update (sent by 3pm, every Friday a deal is in Rehab)

Data the VA gathers Friday by 11am (SOP in `09-VA-PLAYBOOK/sops`): Budget tab totals, Schedule tab statuses, this week's photos (5–10 selected), Issues added this week, next week's planned work from the contractor, any draw request. ## 5. Resale through the listing machine

A finished flip enters `05-SELLER-SYSTEM` at `Listing Signed` with the partner as the seller (Structure A) or Charles's entity (B/C). Everything applies: Seller Intake Form (the VA already has 90% of it from the Project Tracker), `CB Listing Marketer` kit, 14-day pre-launch, Maximum Exposure Standard on Day 0, 5-stage social. Two additions that only flips get:

- **Before/after content**: the same angles photographed at acquisition and at completion. Carousel and Reel at Just Listed; this is the highest-performing listing content there is and it markets the capital partner program at the same time.
- **The renovation sheet** in the property page and MLS attachments: what was done, by whom (licensed), permits closed, warranties. Buyers pay for certainty.

Monday seller reports go to the partner. Day 14 / Day 30 price reviews apply, with the holding-cost math shown ("each additional month costs $[X]; a $[Y] price adjustment now likely nets more than 60 more days").

## 7. What white glove looks like from the partner's chair

- They said yes to a memo. Everything else came to them.
- They got a Friday email every single week, even the boring weeks ("nothing changed, here are the photos").
- They heard bad news by phone first, with a plan attached.
- Their finished home was marketed like a luxury listing, and they saw the reports.
- Their statement arrived before they thought to ask for it.
- The next deal came with "you have first look."
