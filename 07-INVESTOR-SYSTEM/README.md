# 07 – Fix & Flip Capital Partner Program

**The model: Charles brings the deal, capital partners bring the money, Charles runs the project and sells the finished home.**

This is the investor side of the business, rebuilt around that promise. It is the highest-leverage part of the MREA model for Charles because one flip produces up to **three paydays and two listings' worth of marketing**: the acquisition (commission where applicable), the project (fee or profit share per the agreement), and the resale listing (launched at the full Maximum Exposure Standard, which also feeds buyer leads and Just Sold marketing). It also makes Charles the person in [MARKET] that deal sources call first.

White glove, for a capital partner, means: **they never have to find, analyze, manage, or sell anything.** They review a Deal Memo, say yes, wire, receive a Friday update every week, and receive a distribution statement at the end.

## The partner promise (put this on the Invest page and in the onboarding packet)

1. You only see deals that pass a written underwriting standard. We pass on most of what we look at, and we tell you what we passed on and why.
2. Every deal comes with a Deal Memo: the numbers, the comps, the scope of work, the timeline, the exit plan, the risks, and the ask. Nothing is hidden.
3. Tier 1 partners get a 48-hour first look before anyone else.
4. While your capital is in a project, you get a written update every Friday with budget, schedule, photos, and issues.
5. The finished home is sold through the same listing machine we use for every client: professional media, full exposure, weekly reporting.
6. You receive a distribution statement within 5 business days of closing, and a call about the next deal.
7. Nothing is guaranteed. Every number is an estimate you verify. Terms are set by an attorney-drafted agreement, not by a text message.
