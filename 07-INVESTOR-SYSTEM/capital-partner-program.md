# Capital Partner Program

## 1. The offer in one paragraph

"I find and fully underwrite fix & flip opportunities in [MARKET]. I walk every property, price the rehab with licensed contractors, and confirm the resale value with real comps. When one passes, I send you a Deal Memo. If you want in, you fund it under an agreement our attorneys draft, I manage the project, I sell the finished home through my listing system, and you get a written update every Friday until the check clears. You bring the capital. I bring everything else."

## 2. Three ways a partner can participate (details in `structures-and-compliance.md`)

| Structure | Partner does | Charles does | Partner earns | Charles earns |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A. Partner owns the deal** | Buys the property in their name or entity, funds rehab | Sources, underwrites, represents them as buyer's agent, project-manages, lists the resale | The flip profit | Commission on acquisition (where a commission is offered) + resale listing commission + optional project management fee per agreement |
| **B. Joint venture** | Funds acquisition and rehab as the money partner | Sources, manages, sells, may co-invest | Preferred return and/or profit split per JV agreement | Profit split per JV agreement + resale listing commission (disclosed) |
| **C. Private lender** | Lends against the property (first lien), Charles or his entity is the borrower/owner | Everything else | Interest and points | The flip profit after debt service |

Default recommendation for the first 3 deals: **Structure A.** It is the simplest, keeps Charles squarely inside his license, and still delivers the white-glove experience. Move to B or C only with an attorney and once the machine has produced results.

## 3. Partner tiers (tag exactly one)

| Tier | Requirements | What they get |
|---|---|---|
| `Tier-1` | Capital Partner Profile complete, proof of funds or lender letter on file, buyer representation agreement signed (Structure A), can decide in 48 hours | 48-hour first look on every deal, personal call from Charles on each deal, quarterly portfolio call |
| `Tier-2` | Profile complete, funds not yet verified or slower decisions | Deal Alert after the Tier-1 window closes, monthly "deals we passed on" email |
| `Tier-3` | Interested, profile incomplete | Monthly recap only, invitation to complete onboarding |

Rule: **first look goes to the first Tier-1 partner whose profile matches and who replies "I'm in" in writing.** Order of reply, not size of check. Say this out loud in onboarding; it removes every awkward conversation later.

## 5. Onboarding packet (sent by Command at `Partner Onboarded`)

1. Welcome letter (one page): the promise, the tiers, the first-look rule, what happens next.
2. "How a deal flows" one-pager: Source → Underwrite → Deal Memo → 48-hour window → Fund → Rehab (Friday updates) → List → Sell → Distribute → Next deal.
3. Sample Deal Memo (a real closed or anonymized deal) so they know exactly what they will receive.
4. Underwriting standard (the "we pass on most deals" page): minimum profit, maximum purchase (70% rule), rehab contingency, holding assumptions, exit rules.
5. The documents needed for their tier: proof of funds, entity docs, buyer representation agreement (Structure A), attorney contact.
6. The Friday Project Update sample.
7. FAQ: "What if the rehab runs over?", "What if it does not sell?", "Who holds title?", "How are you paid?", "Can I visit the property?", "Can I bring a partner?"

`CB Investor Analyst` drafts every page from the prompts in `03-CHATGPT/prompt-library.md`; the VA designs them in Canva as one branded PDF.

## 6. Underwriting standard (the promise you make and keep)

A deal is presented only if all of these are true. Print this in the onboarding packet.

| Standard | Rule |
|---|---|
| Purchase price | ≤ 70% of ARV minus rehab (the 70% rule), or a stated reason it still works |
| ARV | 3+ sold comps within 0.5 mile and 6 months, similar size and finish, reviewed by Charles in person |
| Rehab estimate | Line-item scope, 2+ contractor bids for anything over $25k, +15% contingency included |
| Holding costs | Modeled at the realistic timeline + 30 days |
| Minimum profit | The greater of $[MIN PROFIT] or [MIN ROI]% on total cash in (Charles sets these once) |
| Exit plan | Primary: resale through Charles at full Maximum Exposure Standard. Backup: rent and refinance, numbers shown |
| Risk section | Every deal lists at least 3 risks and the mitigation for each |
| Disclaimer | Every page: estimates, partner verifies, terms per attorney-drafted agreement |

## 7. How partners find us

- charlesbrewer.guru **Invest** page (`08-WEBSITE-FUNNEL/landing-page-copy.md`) with the sample Deal Memo as the lead magnet.
- Social: monthly "deal we passed on and why" post, project-progress Reels (before/during/after), Just Sold flip posts with the timeline. These make the sourcing engine visible and attract both partners and deal sources.
- Sphere: the 33 Touch includes one annual "I run a capital partner program" item of value. Doctors, business owners, retirees with cash, and past clients who sold at a gain are the list.
- Deal sources double as partner sources: lenders and attorneys know who has money.
- Local REIA / meetups: Charles presents "how I underwrite a flip" once a quarter.
