# Deal Memo (partner-facing package)

This is what a capital partner receives. Fixed order, every time, so a Tier-1 partner can decide in 48 hours because they know exactly where to look. `CB Investor Analyst` drafts every section from the underwriting template; the VA lays it out in the Canva "Deal Memo" template as a PDF; Charles records a 5-minute Loom walking the property and the numbers.

Deliverable set for one deal:
1. **Deal Memo PDF** (8–12 pages, structure below)
2. **Loom video** (5 min: exterior, interior walk, scope, numbers, the ask)
3. **Deal Alert email** (the cover note with the 48-hour window)
4. **Comps and bids folder link** (Drive `02-Comps`, `03-Scope-Bids`) for anyone who wants the raw files

## Pages 2–3: The property

- 8–12 photos (current condition), a plat or aerial, the floor plan sketch
- Facts: beds, baths, sqft, lot, year, construction, roof/HVAC/electrical/plumbing ages, occupancy, how it was sourced
- Condition summary in plain language
- Why the seller is selling and what the negotiation looked like (motivation, timeline)

## Page 4: ARV and comps

- Comps table: address, distance, sale date, price, $/sqft, beds/baths, sqft, condition, adjustments, adjusted value
- Charles's ARV conclusion and the reasoning (which comp is the best match and why)
- Active competition in the price band and its DOM
- The resale buyer profile described by product, not people ("renovated 3/2 under $350k in a neighborhood with < 30 average DOM")

## Page 5: Scope of work and budget

- Line-item scope by category (exterior, kitchen, baths, flooring, paint, systems, permits, landscaping, staging) with bid amounts and contractor names
- Contingency line, and what it is for
- Before/after finish level (photos of a comparable finished flip)
- What is deliberately not being done and why

## Page 6: Timeline

Gantt-style table: contract → inspection → close → permits → demo → rough-ins → drywall → finishes → punch → photos → list → under contract → close. Weeks per phase, milestones that trigger a partner text.

## Page 7: The numbers in full

The complete underwriting from `deal-analysis-template.md`: all formulas, the sensitivity table, the stress case, the backup rental exit, the assumptions register.

## Page 8: Exit plan

- Primary: resale listing through Charles at the Maximum Exposure Standard (pro media, 3D, floor plan, property page, 5-stage social with before/after, database blast, weekly reports). Target list price and the pricing strategy.
- Backup: rent and refinance numbers, or wholesale/assign, or hold longer. When each triggers.
- Who pays what at resale (commission disclosed).

## Page 9: Risks and mitigations

At least three, in a table: Risk | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation | Who bears it under this structure.

## Page 10: The ask and the structure

- Capital needed: amount and date for acquisition, draw schedule for rehab
- Structure offered (A, B, or C), in plain language, with the reminder that the attorney-drafted agreement governs
- How Charles is compensated on this deal (commission on purchase if applicable, resale listing commission, fee or split per agreement). **Always disclosed.**
- What happens next after "I'm in": confirmation call, agreement, title, EMD, timeline

## Page 11: Reporting you will receive

Friday Project Update sample, milestone texts, Monday seller reports during resale, distribution statement sample.

## Page 12: Disclaimers and disclosures

Estimates disclaimer, no guaranteed returns, Charles's licensed status with [BROKERAGE OFFICE], any interest Charles holds in the deal, advice to consult the partner's own attorney, CPA, and lender.

## Quality check before the memo goes out (VA, then Charles)

- Every number on the Snapshot matches page 7
- Comps are renovated-condition sales, dated, within range
- Rehab bids attached, contingency included
- Stress case shown and clears $0
- Charles's compensation disclosed
- No language that guarantees or promises a return, "safe", "risk-free", "can't lose"
- Fair Housing: neighborhood described by market data only
- Disclaimer on every page footer
- Deal folder links resolve; Loom is unlisted and plays
