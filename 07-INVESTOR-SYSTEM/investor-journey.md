# Capital Partner Journey: Touch by Touch

Pipeline: `Cultivate → Criteria Call → Partner Onboarded → Deal Presented → Deal Committed → Acquisition Under Contract → Rehab → Listed → Sold → Repeat`

SmartPlans: `SP-Speed to Lead` (web/social entry), `SP-Capital Partner Program` (onboarding and nurture), `SP-Buyer Under Contract` (acquisition, Structure A), `SP-Seller Listing Launch` and `SP-Seller Under Contract` (resale), `SP-Past Client Program` (after Sold).

Rule: `CB Investor Analyst` drafts, Charles approves, Command sends. Every number carries the estimates disclaimer.

Entry: Invest page form (`Src-Website`), social DM (`Src-Social`), referral (`Src-Referral`), sphere conversation (`Src-Sphere`), REIA/meetup (`Src-Event`). Tags at creation: `Investor`, `New-Lead`, `Haven't Met` (or `Met`), source tag, `Tier-3`.

## Phase 2: Criteria Call (20–30 minutes, Charles)

Opening: "The goal today is for me to understand what you want your money doing, so I only send you deals worth opening. I will explain how I find and underwrite deals, how the first-look window works, and what I need from you to move fast. Then you decide if this is a fit."

Questions (fill the Capital Partner Profile in `capital-partner-program.md` §4 as you go):

1. "Have you funded or flipped before? What went well, what did not?"
2. "How much capital do you want deployed per deal, and in total this year?"
3. "Do you want to own the deal outright with me as your agent and project manager, partner on it, or lend on it?" Explain the three structures in one sentence each. Do not quote splits or rates; say "our attorneys set the terms in writing."
4. "What return makes this worth your time?" (Profit per deal, ROI, or annualized.)
5. "How long can capital be tied up without it being a problem?" (Hold tolerance.)
6. "When I send a deal, how fast can you say yes or no?" Explain the 48-hour first look and the order-of-reply rule.
7. "Where do you want to own, and where would you never own? I evaluate areas on resale velocity, price bands, and rehab risk." (Fair Housing applies: never characterize areas by the people in them.)
8. "Property types and rehab tolerance: cosmetic, full gut, structural?"
9. "Where does the capital sit: cash, HELOC, IRA, lender? Can you get me proof of funds this week?"
10. "Who else is in the decision?"
11. "Do you want to visit projects, or just get the Friday updates?"

Explain the promise (README §"The partner promise"). Explain the underwriting standard. Close: "I will send the recap, your profile, and the onboarding packet today. Once proof of funds is on file you are Tier 1 and you see every deal first."

Exit: VA moves to `Partner Onboarded` when the profile is complete and the packet is sent.

## Phase 7: Listed → Sold

The finished flip is a listing. It runs the entire `05-SELLER-SYSTEM`: the Listing Marketer kit, the Maximum Exposure Standard on Day 0, the 5-stage social sequence (with the before/after story, which performs better than any other listing content), Monday seller reports **sent to the partner**, Day 14 / Day 30 price reviews, offer presentation, `SP-Seller Under Contract` milestones.

## Lost or passed

Partner passes on 3 consecutive deals → Charles calls to re-tune the profile or re-tier. Partner goes silent 90 days → `Tier-3`, monthly recap only, never removed (MREA: nobody is dead, they are just not ready).
