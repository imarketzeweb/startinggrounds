# Structures and Compliance

This file is a checklist for the conversation with a real estate attorney and a securities attorney, not legal advice. **Do not present a Structure B or C deal to anyone until the attorney has drafted the documents and reviewed this program.** Structure A can start now within ordinary licensed activity, subject to brokerage policy.

## 1. The three structures, in detail

### A. Partner owns the deal (start here)

- Partner (or their LLC) buys the property. Charles represents them as buyer's agent under a signed buyer representation agreement (covers off-market deals too).
- Partner contracts directly with contractors, or Charles's entity provides project management under a separate written project management agreement with a flat fee or percentage of rehab.
- Charles lists the finished home under a listing agreement.
- Money never passes through Charles. Partner controls their own funds and title.
- Attorney reviews: the buyer rep agreement wording for off-market/wholesale deals, the project management agreement, any finder's fee to deal sources, and state rules on unlicensed activity for the VA on project tasks.

### B. Joint venture

- A single-purpose LLC per deal (or a JV agreement) with the partner as money member and Charles (or his entity) as manager/operating member.
- Terms an attorney will define: capital contributions, preferred return, profit split, decision rights, who signs contracts, reporting obligations (the Friday update becomes a contractual duty, which is good), what happens on overruns, buy-out and default provisions, dissolution.
- **Securities question:** a passive money partner in an LLC managed by Charles can be a security. One partner per deal who is actively involved in decisions looks different from pooling several passive investors. The attorney decides whether an exemption (for example Regulation D) and accredited-investor limits apply, and what may be said publicly. Until then, **no general solicitation language about JV returns on the website or social**. The Invest page describes the program and collects interest; it does not offer securities.
- Brokerage: disclose Charles's ownership interest on every transaction document and to the other side, per state law and [BROKERAGE OFFICE] policy.

### C. Private lender

- Partner lends to Charles's entity secured by a recorded first-position deed of trust/mortgage, promissory note, personal guarantee if required, lender named on insurance, title policy.
- Terms the attorney defines: rate, points, term, extensions, default interest, draw procedure, servicing.
- Lending laws vary by state (usury caps, licensing thresholds for repeat private lenders). The attorney confirms.
- Charles's entity owns the property; Charles carries the market risk. Only do this once the machine has several Structure A results.

## 2. Compliance rules baked into every document

| Rule | Where enforced |
|---|---|
| No guaranteed, promised, or "safe" returns; every figure is an estimate the partner verifies | Deal Memo footer, Deal Alert, Project Update, distribution statement, Invest page, GPT instructions |
| "Terms per attorney-drafted agreement" on anything discussing structure or splits | same |
| Charles's compensation on the deal disclosed in the memo and in transaction documents | Deal Memo page 10, closing docs |
| Any ownership interest Charles holds disclosed to all parties | Contracts, MLS remarks where required ("agent-owned") |
| Fair Housing: areas described by market data, never by the people in them; the resale marketing follows the seller-system Fair Housing checklist | Deal Memo, listing kit |
| Wire instructions confirmed by phone with the title company before any wire | Journey Phase 5, VA SOP |
| Finder's or referral fees to deal sources only where legal for the recipient's license status, and disclosed | Deal sourcing engine §5 |
| VA performs no activity restricted to licensees (negotiation, advice, contract explanation beyond reading dates) | VA charter |
| Client documents with account numbers, SSNs, or full contracts never go into ChatGPT | `03-CHATGPT/setup-guide.md` privacy note |

## 3. Questions for the attorney meeting (bring this list)

1. Under Structure A, what must the buyer representation and project management agreements say? Any limits on a licensee taking a project management fee?
2. Does one money partner per deal in a manager-managed LLC create a security in this state? What exemption applies and what may be said on a website or social post?
3. Accredited-investor requirements, if any, for B and C, and the questionnaire to use.
4. Private lending: usury, licensing thresholds, required disclosures.
5. Disclosure obligations for an agent with an ownership interest, in contracts and in MLS.
6. Finder's fees to wholesalers, contractors, and unlicensed individuals: what is allowed.
7. The entity structure: one LLC per deal vs. a series LLC, and how it interacts with the brokerage's policy on agent-owned property.
8. Insurance: vacant property, builder's risk, general liability for the entity, umbrella.
9. Tax: dealer status for repeated flips and how the CPA wants deals tracked.

## 4. What to say before the attorney has signed off

Safe language for the website, social, and conversations:

- "I source and underwrite fix & flip opportunities in [MARKET] and work with capital partners who fund them."
- "Every deal comes with a full Deal Memo. You decide."
- "Structures and terms are set by attorney-drafted agreements."
- "Returns are estimates and are not guaranteed."

Not until cleared: specific preferred returns, splits, interest rates, "typical returns of X%", any past-results claim without the disclaimer, or anything that sounds like an offering to the public.
