# 01 – The Four MREA Models, applied to Charles Brewer Real Estate

Gary Keller's *The Millionaire Real Estate Agent* (MREA) says the business rests on **Three L's**:

| L | Meaning | Where it lives in this repo |
|---|---|---|
| **Leads** | A database you feed and touch systematically | `lead-generation-model.md`, `02-COMMAND-CRM`, `04-AUTOMATIONS/command-smartplans` |
| **Listings** | Lead with sellers; listings create signs, calls, buyers, and leverage of your time | `05-SELLER-SYSTEM` |
| **Leverage** | People, systems, and tools so the business does not depend on your hours | `03-CHATGPT`, `04-AUTOMATIONS`, `09-VA-PLAYBOOK`, `organizational-model.md` |

And it runs on **Four Models**, each of which has its own file here:

1. `economic-model.md` – the numbers: net income goal → GCI → closings → appointments → leads → database size.
2. `lead-generation-model.md` – how the database is built and touched (8x8, 33 Touch, 12 Direct) and the lead sources we run.
3. `budget-model.md` – lead with revenue, spend on what produces, "red light / green light".
4. `organizational-model.md` – who does what today (Charles + VA + automation) and the MREA hiring path.

Plus the discipline that makes all four work:

5. `time-blocking.md` – the weekly calendar with lead generation protected first.

## How to use these files

- Fill in `economic-model.md` first (or open `10-SCORECARD/economic-model-calculator.html`). Every other number in the business flows from it.
- Review the four models **quarterly**. Review the scorecard (`10-SCORECARD`) **weekly**.
- Upload all five files to the ChatGPT Project so `CB Ops Manager` can reason from your actual numbers.
