# Time Blocking (MREA)

> *"Time block lead generation first, then protect it like a listing appointment with your most important client."* MREA's standard is about **3 hours of lead generation every morning**. Start at 2 if 3 is not realistic yet, and make it sacred.

## 1. Charles's weekly calendar

| Time | Mon | Tue | Wed | Thu | Fri | Sat | Sun |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 7:00–7:30 | Plan the day, review scorecard dashboard, read overnight leads | same | same | same | same | Off / optional open house prep | Off |
| 7:30–8:00 | **Huddle with VA (15 min)** + approve overnight ChatGPT drafts | same | same | same | same | | |
| 8:00–11:00 | **LEAD GENERATION** (calls, texts, notes, 10-neighbor calls, referral asks) | LEAD GEN | LEAD GEN | LEAD GEN | LEAD GEN (until 10:00) | | |

| 1:30–5:30 | **APPOINTMENTS** (listing consults, buyer consults, showings) | APPOINTMENTS | APPOINTMENTS | APPOINTMENTS | APPOINTMENTS (light) | Open house 12–3 (when hosting) or showings | Optional showings 1–4 |

| Evening | Family. Phone on Do Not Disturb except `#leads` alerts until 8pm | | | | | | |

Rules:
1. Lead generation blocks are **appointments with the business**. Only a live client emergency moves them, and they get rescheduled the same day.
2. Appointments cluster in the afternoons so mornings stay clean. The VA books consults only into the 1:30–5:30 blocks via Calendly.
3. Two approval windows a day mean ChatGPT drafts never wait more than half a business day.
4. One content batch every other Tuesday produces 4 videos = 2 weeks of social plus future YouTube raw material.
5. Friday afternoon is planning, not prospecting. Next week's calendar is set before the weekend.

## 2. What happens in the lead generation block

| Minutes | Activity | Count | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–60 | Speed-to-lead callbacks and new lead calls (anything from last 24 hrs first) | all | Command "New-Lead" filter |
| 60–120 | 33 Touch call tasks due today + past client check-ins | 8–10 conversations | Command SmartPlan tasks |
| 120–150 | 10 neighbor calls around any Coming Soon / Just Listed / Just Sold | 10 dials | Listing address list from VA |
| 150–180 | Handwritten notes (3) and personal video messages (2) | 5 | Names surfaced during calls |

Every conversation ends with: *"Who do you know that is thinking about buying, selling, or investing in [MARKET] this year?"* Log every conversation in Command before the next dial. The VA cleans up anything missed at the 1:00 database hygiene block.
