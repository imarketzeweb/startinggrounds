# Budget Model

> MREA principle: *"Lead with revenue."* Spend on lead generation and leverage in proportion to the revenue they produce, and play **red light / green light**: when the numbers are green (revenue on plan) spend the next dollar; when they are red, hold.

## 1. The two buckets

MREA separates money into **Cost of Sales** (money you pay only because a deal closed) and **Operating Expenses** (money you pay to keep the doors open). Track them separately in the scorecard.

| Bucket | Items | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost of Sales** | Brokerage split/royalty until cap, referral fees paid, buyer-agent or listing-specialist splits (future), transaction coordinator per-file fee (if outsourced) | Variable, scales with closings |
| **Operating Expenses** | Salaries (VA), lead generation, technology, occupancy, phone, supplies, education, equipment, auto, insurance, professional fees | Budgeted monthly, reviewed weekly |

## 3. Red light / green light rules

| Signal | Green (spend the next dollar) | Red (hold) |
|---|---|---|
| Appointments per week (trailing 4 weeks) | ≥ 2 | < 1.5 |
| GCI year-to-date vs plan | ≥ 90% | < 80% |
| Cost per appointment by source | Below GCI-per-side ÷ 10 | Rising two months in a row |
| Cash reserve | ≥ 3 months operating expenses | < 2 months |

Spending order when green (MREA priority):
1. More lead generation in the source with the best cost per appointment.
2. More leverage (VA hours → transaction coordinator → buyer specialist).
3. Better tools.

Never cut when red: listing marketing standards (see `05-SELLER-SYSTEM/listing-launch-playbook.md`), the VA, database touches. Cut first: paid ads, unused software, events with no measured leads.

## 4. Per-listing marketing budget (Cost of Sales mindset)

Treat each listing as a project with its own budget. Typical:

| Item | Typical cost | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Professional photos (25–40, incl. twilight) | $200–400 | Yes |
| Drone stills + video | $150–300 | Yes (where legal) |
| 3D tour (Matterport / Zillow 3D Home) | $0–300 | Yes |
| 2D floor plan | $50–150 (often bundled) | Yes |
| 60–90s walkthrough video edit | $0–250 | Yes |
| Virtual staging (vacant only) | $30–75 per photo | When vacant |
| Sign + rider + QR | $50–150 | Yes |
| Single-property page | $0–50 | Yes |
| Open house supplies + neighbor mailer (100 homes) | $100–200 | Yes |
| Social boost of Just Listed post | $50–100 | Recommended |
| **Total per listing** | **$700–1,700** | |

At a $10,000 average GCI per side, that is 7–17% of the listing's revenue, and it is what makes the listing viewable, sells it faster, and produces the buyer and neighbor leads that lower the cost of the next deal.

## 5. Monthly money ritual (30 minutes, first business day)

1. Bookkeeper sends P&L by category above.
2. VA updates the "Money" tab of the scorecard: GCI closed, GCI pending, cost of sales, operating expenses by category, cost per lead and per appointment by source.
3. Charles marks each category green/yellow/red.
4. Decide one spend to add and one to cut. Write it in the scorecard notes.
5. `CB Ops Manager` prompt: "Here is this month's P&L and scorecard. Summarize in 5 bullets: what produced, what did not, what to change."
