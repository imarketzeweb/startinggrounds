# Organizational Model

> MREA principle: *"Leverage is people, systems, and tools."* Hire in the order that frees the rainmaker's time to do the two things only he can do: **generate leads and go on appointments.**
> First hire is administrative. You already made it. The next hires are earned by hitting the economic model.

## 2. Role charter

### Charles (rainmaker, licensed)

Does only what requires his license, his voice, or his judgment:

| Category | Tasks | Time blocked |
|---|---|---|
| Lead generation | Database calls/texts, 10 neighbor calls, referral asks, investor criteria calls, personal video messages | 2–3 hrs daily, mornings |
| Appointments | Listing consults, buyer consults, investor reviews | Afternoons/evenings |
| Pricing and negotiation | CMAs (VA preps, Charles decides), offers, counters, inspection negotiations | As needed |
| Client relationship moments | Listing signed call, offer presentation, closing day, the 7-day post-close call | As needed |
| Approvals | Anything a client sees that ChatGPT drafted (marketing kit, weekly seller reports, lead replies) | 2 × 20 min daily approval windows |
| Content | Records 1 face-to-camera video per week (batch 4 per month), reads/approves social captions | 90 min weekly |
| Leadership | Daily 15-min huddle with VA, Friday scorecard review, monthly money ritual, quarterly model review | ~3 hrs weekly |

### Virtual Assistant (unlicensed, remote)

Full charter in `09-VA-PLAYBOOK/README.md`. In MREA terms the VA currently covers **four seats**: lead coordinator, listing/marketing coordinator, transaction coordinator, and database/admin. As volume grows these split into separate hires.

Never does: give pricing or legal advice, negotiate, discuss contract terms with a client, or perform any activity your state or brokerage restricts to licensees. Confirm the list with [BROKERAGE OFFICE].

### Automation layer (Zapier + Command)

Owns: instant acknowledgment, contact creation and tagging, SmartPlan enrollment, task creation, alerts, stage-driven triggers, scheduled reminders, reporting rollups. Documented in `04-AUTOMATIONS`.

### ChatGPT layer

Owns: first drafts of everything written, qualification suggestions, deal math, SOP writing, weekly summaries. Documented in `03-CHATGPT`. Never sends anything itself.

## 4. The MREA hiring path from here

Hire when the trailing-90-day numbers say the current structure is the bottleneck, not when it feels busy.

| Step | Hire | Trigger (from scorecard) | What it frees |
|---|---|---|---|
| Done | **Admin / VA** | | Charles's time for lead gen and appointments |
| Next | **VA hours → full-time, or second VA (marketing-only)** | Social posting slips 2 weeks in a row, or seller reports go out late twice, or listing launches miss the Day 0 standard | Consistency of marketing and listing quality |
| 2 | **Transaction coordinator** (per-file contractor is fine) | > 4 files under contract at once for 2 months | 5–8 hrs per file of paperwork and vendor chasing |
| 3 | **Buyer specialist** (licensed) | Charles is turning down or delaying buyer showings; buyer sides > 20/yr; listing appointments are being missed because of showings | Charles's afternoons for listing appointments (MREA: rainmaker keeps listings, hands off buyers first) |
| 4 | **Showing assistant / second buyer specialist** | Buyer specialist > 25 closings | |
| 5 | **Listing specialist** | Listing appointments > 6/week and Charles wants to move to lead-gen-and-leadership only | The path to a business that runs without you |
| 6 | **Lead coordinator / ISA** | > 40 new leads/week from web/social/paid | Speed to lead at scale |
| 7 | **Marketing director** | 3+ listings/month plus YouTube (see `11-YOUTUBE-LATER`) | Content and brand at scale |

Job descriptions are drafted with `CB Ops Manager` (prompt in `03-CHATGPT/prompt-library.md`). Every hire gets: a written role charter like the ones above, a 30/60/90 plan, weekly KPIs on the scorecard, and SOPs from `09-VA-PLAYBOOK/sops`.

## 5. Systems before people

MREA's rule: **document the job before you hire for it.** That is what this repo is. Every seat above already has its SOPs, checklists, and GPTs, so the next hire inherits a working system instead of building one.
