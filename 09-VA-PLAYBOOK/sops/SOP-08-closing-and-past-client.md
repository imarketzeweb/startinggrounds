# SOP-08  Closing and Past Client

## Purpose
Service standard 5: every closed client enters `SP-Past Client Program` the day of closing, gets a closing gift, a personal call from Charles, a review request at Day 7, and a home anniversary reminder forever. On capital partner deals, the sold flip also triggers the distribution statement, partner review request, and next-deal call (Z-16). Z-09 does the mechanical part; this SOP is the human part and the fallback.

## Trigger
- Closing is within 5 business days (gift ordering), and closing day itself (recording confirmed).

## Steps
1. **Day -5: closing gift.** Choose from the gift list (Charles's standard options and budget in `#ops` pinned message; personalize from intake notes: kids, pets, hobby, favorite local place). Order for delivery on closing day or hand delivery by Charles at closing. Log the gift and cost in the Opportunity. (Z-09 also creates this task on closing day; if you already ordered, close the task with "ordered [date]".)
2. **Day -2: review link and card.** Prepare the Google review link (short URL) and a handwritten card blank for Charles to write at closing. Confirm the client's preferred contact channel for the Day 7 request (email vs. text).
3. **Closing day: confirm and move.** When title confirms recording (and funding/keys for buyers), move the Opportunity to `Closed` in Command and set the sheet stage to Closed with the closing date, price, and source (fires Z-09). Post `Closed: [name], [address]` in `#ops`.
4. **Closing day: tags (verify Z-09 or do by hand).** Add `Past Client`, `Closed`, `Met`, `Past-Client-Program`. Remove `Buyer` or `Seller` (keep `Investor` and `Capital-Partner` for partners), `Active`, `Under-Contract`, `Hot`, `New-Lead`, `Nurture`, `A`/`B`/`C`, `Buyer-Nurture`, `Seller-Nurture`, `8x8-Active`. Never change `Src-*`. Remove the under-contract SmartPlan; start `SP-Past Client Program`. Set the Home Anniversary field (or note "Closed [date] at [address]"). Confirm the yearly calendar event exists for Charles and you.
5. **Closing day: messages.** Charles sends the closing-day text himself. You send the Day 2 documents-and-checklist email from the plan after confirming the documents link (title's closing package uploaded to `05-Docs` or the buyer folder).
6. **Day 1: thank-you call.** Charles's task. Remind him at the huddle.
7. **Day 3: gift delivered.** Confirm delivery; log it; close the task.
8. **Day 7: review request.** Send the Day 7 email (or text, if that is their preference) from Command with the review link after Charles's approval of the template (one-time approval; after that you send). Log "review requested [date]" in the contact. If a review posts, Z-11 runs; thank them in the thread and add the testimonial card to the social queue.
9. **Day 14: no review yet.** One friendly text: "Hi [First Name], [VA NAME] from Charles's team. If you had two minutes for a quick Google review, it means a lot to a small business: [link]. Thank you either way!" Then stop; never ask a third time.
10. **Day 30 and beyond.** `SP-Past Client Program` tasks arrive; fill placeholders, post for approval where required, send from Command. Log every reply. Any mention of a friend moving is a referral lead: `SOP-01` with `Src-Referral`, and a thank-you task for Charles.
11. **Sellers: neighbors.** Send the Just Sold email to the neighbor list via Command Campaign after Charles approves (copy from the kit). Confirm the Just Sold post went out (Z-07).
12. **Capital partner deals (flip sold).** Set Deal Board status to Sold (fires Z-16). Gather the final closing statement into `06-Closing`; give Charles the distribution inputs (sale price, all-in costs, splits, each partner's capital). Do not calculate or send distributions yourself; Charles verifies and the accountant reviews. After approval, send each partner their closing email and statement individually from Command, log it, then the Day 10 partner review request. Move each partner's Investor Opportunity to `Repeat` and remove the `Deal-[ID]` tag.
13. **Scorecard.** Closed unit, volume, GCI, source, and review-request-sent onto the KPI sheet Friday (`SOP-10`).

## Done-when
- Opportunity Closed, tags correct, `SP-Past Client Program` running, gift delivered and logged, review requested Day 7, anniversary reminder set, scorecard updated; for flips, distribution statement approved and sent, partners at `Repeat`.

## Time
- 90 minutes across closing week per client; 2 hours extra for a flip's partner closing.

## Tools
- KW Command, `Active Listings` / `Active Buyers` / `Deal Board` / `Capital Partners` sheets, Google Calendar, Google review link, gift vendors, Slack `#ops`, Google Drive.

## Escalate-if
- Closing is delayed, funding fails, or recording does not happen on the day: Charles calls the client; you update the tracker and do nothing else in writing.
- The client is unhappy at closing (walk-through issue, funds discrepancy): no review request until Charles says so.
- Any partner asks about their distribution, taxes, or the next deal's terms: Charles.
- A past client mentions a problem with the home after closing (defect, dispute with the other party): Charles the same day; no advice from you.
