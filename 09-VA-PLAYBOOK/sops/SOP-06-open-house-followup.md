# SOP-06  Open House Follow-up

## Purpose
Open house visitors are the highest-converting leads we get, but only if every one is in Command the same day with `Src-OpenHouse` and `SP-8x8 New Contact`, and gets a personal text from Charles before bedtime. This SOP covers the setup before, the sign-in during, and the follow-up after. Promo, neighbor invites, and day-of flow are in `05-SELLER-SYSTEM/open-house-system.md`; Z-04 does the mechanical part.

## Trigger
- Charles or the seller confirms an open house date (put it in `Active Listings` and the `Open House Schedule` sheet the same day).

## Steps
1. **7 days before: promo.** Schedule the Open House posts (3 days and 1 day before, plus day-of story) per `SOP-04`. Build the neighbor invite list (Charles's door-knock and call script from the kit). Add the open house to the MLS and portals. Update the Z-05 lookup row (next open house date) so sign leads see it.
2. **3 days before: sign-in form.** Copy the `Open House Sign-in [ADDRESS]` Google Form template; rename with the address; add the row to `Open House Schedule` (form name, address, listing Opportunity link, date). Test one submission end to end: contact in Command with `Src-OpenHouse` and `Met`, `SP-8x8 New Contact` started, Slack alert with text draft. Delete the test contact. Set up the iPad in kiosk mode with the form; print a paper sign-in sheet as backup and 20 property flyers with the QR code.
3. **Day before.** Confirm with the seller (be out by [time], lights, pets), confirm Charles has the flyers and sign-in iPad, post the "tomorrow" story, remind the neighbor list by text if Charles approved the script.
4. **During (if attending or remote).** Every visitor signs in before touring; if the wifi drops, entries queue or go on paper. Watch `#leads` for sign-in alerts; if Z-04 fails, type paper entries into the form that evening.
5. **Same day, by 7:00pm: follow-up drafts.** For each sign-in alert, review the Z-04 text draft: right street, right question for their answers, under 280 characters. Fix anything off. Post "Open house drafts ready: [count]" in `#leads`. Charles approves in bulk (thumbs-up per thread or "all approved"). Send each approved text from Command by 8:00pm the same day. Log "texted" in each thread.
6. **Same evening: Command.** For every visitor: tags `Src-OpenHouse`, `Met`, type (`Buyer` unless they said they are selling, then `Seller`; `Sphere` if working with another agent and not interested), `New-Lead`, temperature from their timeline (0 to 30 days = A). Note: which home, working with agent, interested, timeline, anything they said. Buyer Opportunity at `Cultivate` for anyone interested or with a 0 to 90 day timeline. `SP-8x8 New Contact` running (add if Z-04 could not).
7. **Next morning.** Any visitor who replied to the text: human reply within 5 minutes per `SOP-01` rules (Charles or you). Anyone interested in the home: Charles calls before noon; book the second showing. Anyone with a home to sell: Charles calls; Seller Opportunity created.
8. **Seller report.** Add attendance, sign-in count, and first impressions (no names) to the open house tab so Monday's report (`SOP-05`) includes it. Text the seller the headcount the same evening if Charles has not already (you may send: "Hi [First Name], [VA NAME]. [X] groups came through today. Charles will share impressions Monday in your report, and sooner if there's anything urgent.").
9. **Two days later.** Any visitor who has not replied: the `SP-8x8 New Contact` Day 0 email goes (approve it). Log any bounce or wrong number and correct the contact.
10. **Log.** `Lead Log` rows exist for every sign-in (Z-04 creates them; add any manual ones). Scorecard Friday: open house attendance, leads, and appointments set.

## Done-when
- Every visitor is in Command with correct tags, note, plan, and (where warranted) Opportunity by 9:00pm the same day; every approved same-day text sent by 8:00pm; seller has the headcount; open house tab updated for Monday.

## Time
- 2 hours setup across the week, 1 hour same-day follow-up per 10 sign-ins.

## Tools
- Google Forms (sign-in template), iPad kiosk, `Open House Schedule` and `Active Listings` sheets, KW Command, Slack `#leads`, social scheduler, printer, `Lead Log`.

## Escalate-if
- A visitor says they want to write an offer or asks about price or terms: Charles calls them that evening; do not send the standard text.
- A visitor identifies as a neighbor thinking of selling: tell Charles the same evening; he calls next morning.
- A visitor complains about the home, the seller, or the showing: tell Charles; nothing goes in writing to the seller from you.
- Sign-in data was lost (paper missing, form failure): tell Charles the same evening so he can call whoever he remembers.
