# SOP-03  Listing Launch Coordination

## Purpose
Every listing launches with the Maximum Exposure Standard: pro photos, drone, video walkthrough, 3D tour, floor plan, single-property page, 5-stage social sequence, and an email blast to the database, with a Monday written update from day one. This SOP is the VA's path from "listing signed" to "live and clean." It works alongside `05-SELLER-SYSTEM/listing-launch-playbook.md`, `SP-Seller Listing Launch`, and Zaps Z-06 and Z-07.

## Trigger
- Charles posts "Listing signed: [ADDRESS]" in `#listings`, or the Seller Opportunity moves to `Listing Signed`.

## Steps
1. **Day 0: intake.** Call the seller within 2 hours (Charles has already told them you will). Complete the Seller Intake Form together (all fields in Z-06, including "do not mention" and the seller's story). Submit it. This fires Z-06: Drive folder, Opportunity update, marketing kit draft, launch tasks. If Z-06 fails, create the folder `Listings/[YYYY-MM] [Street Address]/` with `01-Photos`, `02-Video`, `03-3D-FloorPlan`, `04-Marketing-Kit`, `05-Docs` by hand, and run `CB Listing Marketer` yourself with the intake.
2. **Day 0: sheet and plan.** Add the row to `Active Listings` (address, seller, Opportunity link, price, folder link, target live date, stage = Listing Signed, kit_approved = No). Confirm `SP-Seller Listing Launch` is running. Send the step 3 "Meet [VA NAME]" email (you may approve this one).
3. **Day 0 to 2: book media.** Photographer with drone, video walkthrough, Matterport or Zillow 3D Home, floor plan. One visit if possible. Confirm date, time, and access with the seller; send the photo-prep list. Book a cleaner or stager if Charles says so. Put every booking on Charles's calendar.
4. **Day 1 to 3: sign and QR.** Order the yard sign and a QR rider. The QR points to the single-property page with `?utm_source=sign&utm_medium=qr&utm_campaign=[Address]`. Add the address row to the Z-05 lookup (same `Active Listings` sheet: seller name, price, showing instructions, next open house).
5. **Day 2 to 5: marketing kit QA.** Open the Z-06 Doc `[Address] Marketing Kit v1 DRAFT`. Check every section against the Fair Housing checklist in `05-SELLER-SYSTEM/listing-marketing-kit-template.md`: no words about people, families, safety, schools' quality, "walking distance to church", or anything describing who lives nearby. Check every fact against the intake (beds, baths, sqft, year, features). Fix obvious errors, mark anything uncertain with `[VERIFY]`, and post in `#listings`: "Kit v1 ready for review, [X] items to verify." Charles approves in the Doc. Set `kit_approved = Yes` in the sheet only after his approval.
6. **Day 6 to 8: media in.** Download all media into the folder; rename photos in display order (`01-front.jpg`, ...), mark the hero `00-hero.jpg`. Upload video to the unlisted channel and CapCut for cuts. Confirm the 3D tour link and floor plan PDF. Send the step 6 "photos are in" email after Charles approves.
7. **Day 7 to 10: MLS input handoff.** Build the MLS draft (fields, remarks from the approved kit, photos in order, 3D link, floor plan, showing instructions, compensation and terms fields left blank for Charles). Post "MLS draft ready" in `#listings`. Charles reviews and enters or approves every term and the price. The VA never enters price, compensation, or contract terms.
8. **Day 8 to 12: pages and campaigns.** Build the single-property page on charlesbrewer.guru from the approved copy. Build the Command email Campaign (database blast) from the kit, scheduled for launch day 9am, with Charles's approval. Schedule the 5-stage social posts as drafts in the scheduler (`SOP-04`), named `[Address]-[Stage]-[Platform]`. Print open house materials if an open house is booked.
9. **Day 12: QA checklist.** Post in `#listings` with each item checked: MLS draft complete and reviewed by Charles; photos in order, hero set; 3D link works on mobile; floor plan attached; property page live in preview, form tested through Z-05; sign installed with QR tested; social drafts for Coming Soon and Just Listed approved; email Campaign approved and scheduled; showing instructions and lockbox set; `Active Listings` row complete.
10. **Coming Soon and go-live.** When Charles moves the Opportunity to `Coming Soon` and then `Active`, update the sheet stage the same hour (this is the Z-07 trigger if Command's trigger is not available). Approve the Z-07 post drafts in the scheduler after Charles approves in `#listings`. Send the live-day text and email after approval.
11. **Day +1: syndication check.** Confirm the listing on Zillow, Realtor.com, Redfin, Homes.com, and KW sites: photos, price, remarks, 3D link. Report any error to Charles and fix in MLS within the hour.
12. **Ongoing.** Showing feedback relayed to Charles within 24 hours; Monday reports (`SOP-05`); open houses (`SOP-06`); Day 14 and Day 30 review prep for Charles (comps and traffic summary only).

## Done-when
- Listing live with every Maximum Exposure item, QA checklist posted, syndication verified, `Active Listings` row current, seller has received the plan, meet-the-VA, photos, and live-day messages.

## Time
- 6 to 9 hours per listing across 14 days, plus 1 hour weekly while active.

## Tools
- KW Command, Google Drive, `Active Listings` sheet, `CB Listing Marketer`, MLS, photographer/3D vendors, sign vendor, charlesbrewer.guru, social scheduler, Command Designs / Canva, CapCut, Slack `#listings`.

## Escalate-if
- Seller asks about price, commission, offers, or what to disclose: "Charles will call you," then tell him.
- Any media vendor cannot deliver before the target live date: tell Charles the same day; he decides whether to move the date.
- Anything in the intake or photos raises a disclosure question (water damage, permits, unpermitted work): flag to Charles before publishing anything.
- A Fair Housing concern you are not sure about: leave it out and ask.
