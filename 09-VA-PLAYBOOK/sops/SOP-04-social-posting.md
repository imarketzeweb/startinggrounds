# SOP-04  Social Posting

## Purpose
Social media is the content channel now (Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile). This SOP covers building posts from Command Designs and Canva templates, scheduling, UTM links, story sequences, and the 1-hour reply rule for comments and DMs, which is where social leads actually come from.

## Trigger
- Daily 9:00am social block.
- Z-07 posts a stage draft to the scheduler and Charles approves it in `#listings`.
- An open house is booked (Open House stage posts).
- A review is approved for social (Z-11).
- The Tuesday weekly scheduling block.

## Steps
1. **Weekly plan (Tuesday).** Build next week's calendar in the scheduler: minimum 5 posts per week plus every listing stage post. Mix from `05-SELLER-SYSTEM/social-media-listing-templates.md`: 2 listing or market posts, 1 client story or review, 1 local place or event, 1 educational (buying, selling, or renovation), plus Reels/TikTok cuts from listing video. Charles approves the plan at the Monday huddle; listing stage captions are already approved in the kit.
2. **Build.** Use Command Designs templates first; Canva with the brand kit when Designs cannot do it. Name every file `[Address]-[Stage]-[Platform]` for listings, `[Topic]-[Date]-[Platform]` otherwise. Hero images from `01-Photos`; never use MLS photos of another agent's listing.
3. **Captions.** Listing captions come from the approved Marketing Kit Doc, unchanged except for dates. Non-listing captions: draft in `CB Listing Marketer` or `CB Ops Manager` prompts from `03-CHATGPT/prompt-library.md`, then post in `#listings` for approval (Charles may pre-approve categories; note which in this file). Fair Housing check on every caption: property and places, never people.
4. **Links and UTMs.** Every link goes to charlesbrewer.guru with `utm_source=[platform]&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=[Address or Topic]` so Z-01 and Z-05 log the source and the scorecard can attribute leads. Single-property pages in bio link for active listings.
5. **Schedule.** Post times: 7:30am, 12:00pm, or 6:00pm local. Coming Soon: 3 days before live. Just Listed: live day 9:30am. Open House: 3 days and 1 day before, plus a same-day story. Under Contract: after seller permission is noted in the Opportunity. Just Sold: day of closing or next morning. Cross-post IG to FB; LinkedIn gets Just Listed, Just Sold, and market posts only; Google Business Profile gets one post per week (listing or market). Z-07 stage posts arrive as drafts; approve them in the scheduler only after Charles's `#listings` approval.
6. **Stories.** Story sequences: Coming Soon (3 frames: exterior, one interior detail, "DM me for early access"), Open House day (arrival, 2 rooms, "here until [time]", wrap with headcount), Just Sold (keys photo, "congrats [first name only, with permission]"). Save listing stories to a highlight named by street.
7. **Replies (all day, 1-hour rule).** Check comments and DMs at 9:00, 11:00, 1:00, 3:00, and 4:30. Reply to every comment (a like plus a sentence). DMs: answer within 1 hour during business hours; never quote price opinions or anything about offers; for "is it still available", "what's the price", "can I see it": answer the factual part from the sheet, then ask for a name and best number and offer a showing via Charles.
8. **Log leads.** Any DM that gives a name and contact, asks to see a home, or asks about selling is a lead: submit the `DM Lead Capture` form within 5 minutes (fires Z-03), reply in the DM that Charles will call, and post in the `#leads` thread when you have. The 5-minute standard applies from the moment they give you contact details.
9. **Measure.** Friday: reach, followers, saves, DMs answered within 1 hour (count), leads from social (Lead Log `Src-Social`) into the scorecard (`SOP-10`).
10. **Repurpose.** Every listing video gets 2 to 3 short cuts in CapCut with captions burned in; every approved review becomes a testimonial card; every Monday market stat becomes one slide post.

## Done-when
- Week's posts scheduled by Tuesday 5pm; every stage post published within its window; every comment and DM answered within 1 hour; every DM lead logged; Friday social numbers on the scorecard.

## Time
- 45 minutes daily, 2 hours Tuesday scheduling, 15 minutes per listing stage post, 1 hour per video cut session.

## Tools
- Command Designs, Canva, CapCut, social scheduler (Buffer / Later / Metricool), Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile, `DM Lead Capture` form, `Active Listings` sheet, Slack `#listings` and `#leads`.

## Escalate-if
- A comment or DM is a complaint, mentions Fair Housing, discrimination, another agent, or a legal issue: do not reply; screenshot and send to Charles.
- Someone asks for a price opinion, offer advice, or commission in a DM: "Charles will call you" and log it.
- A post has been live with a factual error (price, beds, address): take it down immediately, then tell Charles.
- Brokerage advertising rules are unclear for a format (logo, office name, license number): ask before posting.
