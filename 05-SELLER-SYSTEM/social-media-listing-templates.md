# Social Media Listing Templates

Platform-by-platform templates for the five listing stages, the weekly non-listing calendar, open house story sequences, and the brand spec. The VA builds and schedules. Charles approves anything with a price or a client's name, and records any on-camera pieces.

Naming: every post file and design is `[Address]-[Stage]-[Platform]` (e.g. `123-Main-JustListed-IG`). Captions come from section 11 of the marketing kit.

Platforms in use: Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, LinkedIn, Google Business Profile. YouTube is parked (see 11-YOUTUBE-LATER) but Shorts are posted anyway since the edit already exists.

## 3. Stage templates

Each stage lists: post type, visual spec, caption template, hashtags, time, CTA, repurposing.

### 3.1 Coming Soon (Day -3 to Day -1)

Goal: DMs and property page sign-ups before it goes live. Check local MLS rules on Coming Soon marketing first. If Coming Soon is not permitted in MLS, social posts still work but no showings.

| Item | Spec |
|---|---|
| Post type | Day -3: teaser Reel (drone reveal, exterior only, no address). Day -2: static or carousel with one interior shot and "Coming Soon" overlay. Day -1: story countdown sticker to launch time |
| Visual | Reel 9:16, 7 to 15s, drone orbit → one interior hero → "Coming Soon to [NEIGHBORHOOD]" end card. Static 4:5, hero photo, top-left "COMING SOON" pill in brand color, bottom "[NEIGHBORHOOD] | [BEDS] bed [BATHS] bath" |
| Caption (IG) | "Coming soon to [NEIGHBORHOOD]. [One-line hook from headline 3]. [BEDS] beds, [BATHS] baths, [SQFT] sqft, and [one standout feature]. Goes live [day]. Want the first look? DM me FIRST and I'll send the details before it hits the market." |
| Caption (FB) | Same, replace DM line with "Comment or message me for early details. Link coming [day]." |
| Caption (LinkedIn) | "Launching a new listing in [NEIGHBORHOOD] this week. [Market angle: inventory in this price band, why this one matters]. Details [day]." |
| Caption (TikTok) | On-screen: "Coming soon to [NEIGHBORHOOD]". Caption: "Wait for the reveal [emoji]. Comment FIRST for early access." |
| Hashtags | Branded 5 + local 8 + #ComingSoon #NewListing #HomeTour |
| Time | Day -3 Reel 7pm. Day -2 static 11am. Day -1 story 8am and 6pm |
| CTA | "DM me FIRST" (VA replies with property page sign-up link, tags contact `Src-Social`, starts `SP-Speed to Lead`) |
| Repurpose | One teaser edit → IG Reel, TikTok, FB Reel, YouTube Short, IG and FB Story (cut to 15s) |

## 5. Weekly non-listing content calendar

The feed has to look alive when there are zero active listings. One post per day, batch-produced on the previous Friday by the VA, Charles records any on-camera pieces in one 45-minute block per week.

| Day | Theme | Format | Template | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Market Minute | 30 to 45s talking head Reel or 3-slide carousel | "[MARKET] this week: [N] new listings, [N] pendings, median [PRICE], days on market [N]. What it means: [one sentence]." | VA pulls MLS stats Monday 8am, Charles records or VA builds carousel |
| Tue | Client Story | Static or carousel | "[First name] wanted [goal]. The challenge was [X]. Here's what we did. Result: [Y]." | Past clients with permission. Rotate buyers, sellers, investors |
| Wed | Neighborhood Spotlight | Reel (phone footage) or carousel | "[NEIGHBORHOOD] in 30 seconds: [coffee], [park], [what it costs], [who it fits: lifestyle, not demographics]." | Builds the local hashtag footprint, reused in listing kits |
| Thu | Tip | Talking head Reel or text carousel | "One thing [MARKET] sellers get wrong: [X]. Do this instead: [Y]." Alternate seller tip, buyer tip, investor tip | Source from `CB Seller Concierge` and `CB Buyer Concierge` FAQ lists |
| Fri | Listing or Just Sold | Per stage templates above | If no listing: "Sold this year" recap or a coming soon tease for the pipeline | Never skip Friday. If nothing, post a past sale with a lesson |
| Sat | Lifestyle | Static or story series | Local event, restaurant, trail, farmers market. "Where to be in [MARKET] this weekend." | Tag the business. Builds local goodwill and Src-Social leads |
| Sun | Behind the Scenes | Story series or short Reel | Prep for the week, a look at how a launch comes together, the VA's view, a client gift being packed | Human, low polish, phone footage |

Monthly additions: one long-form LinkedIn post (market outlook), one GBP post per week minimum (reuse Mon or Thu content), one "ask me anything" story on the last Sunday.

## 6. Open house story sequences

Run on Instagram and Facebook Stories. Each sticker result goes into the open house notes.

| When | Story | Sticker | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| OH minus 4 | Hero photo, "Open house this [day]" | Location, link to property page | Awareness |
| OH minus 3 | Two photos side by side | Poll: "Kitchen or backyard, which do you want to see first?" | Engagement, tells you what to feature |
| OH minus 2 | Floor plan | Quiz: "How many bedrooms?" | Engagement |
| OH minus 2 | Charles on camera | Q&A box: "Ask me anything about [NEIGHBORHOOD] or this house" | Leads: every question gets a DM reply and a `Src-Social` contact |
| OH minus 1 | Hero photo | Countdown sticker to the open house start time | Reminder notifications |
| OH minus 1, evening | "What to see tomorrow" 15s Reel shared to story | Link | Reminder |
| OH day, arrival | Sign going out, door open | Location | "We're here" |
| OH day, 30 min in | Best room, quick pan | Poll: "Would you?" | Engagement |
| OH day, midway | Refreshments, neighbors arriving (no faces without permission) | none | Social proof |
| OH day, 30 min left | Charles at the door | "30 minutes left, come through" | Urgency |
| OH plus 1 | Recap Reel | "[N] groups came through. Missed it? DM me TOUR." | Lead capture |

VA responds to every poll, quiz, and question reply within 1 hour during the open house window and adds new contacts to Command with `Src-Social`.
