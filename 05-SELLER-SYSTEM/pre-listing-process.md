# Pre-Listing Process

Covers everything from "come look at the house" to the day the listing is signed, plus the 14-day pre-launch. Pipeline stages: Appointment Set → Appointment Met → Listing Signed.

Owners: Charles runs the calls and the consultation. The VA builds the packet, fills Command, books vendors, and runs `CB Seller Concierge` for prep drafts.

## 1. Intake call script (Charles, 20 minutes, 2 to 3 days before the consultation)

Purpose: fill the Seller Intake Form live, learn the real motivation, set expectations for the consultation. This is not the pricing conversation. If they push for a number: "I'll have a real number for you at our meeting once I've walked the house and pulled the comps. A number today would be a guess and you deserve better than a guess."

```
Open (2 min)
"Thanks for making time. Two goals for this call: I want to learn the house from you, because
nobody knows it better, and I want to make sure our meeting on [date] is worth your time.
I'm going to ask a lot of questions and type while you talk. Okay?"

Motivation (5 min)
- "What's prompting the move?"
- "If everything went perfectly, where are you living on [date 90 days out]?"
- "Is there a date that matters: job start, school, closing on another home, lease?"
- "Have you sold a home before? What did the last agent do well, and what drove you crazy?"
- "Is anyone else involved in the decision?" (spouse, parent, sibling, attorney, co-owner)

The house (8 min)
Walk the Seller Intake Form top to bottom. Ask for dates and rough cost on every upgrade.
"What do you love most about living there?" Write the answer verbatim. It becomes the story.
"What would you change if you were staying?" That is the objection list.
"Anything a buyer will find that we should talk about first?" (disclosures, quirks, neighbors)

Logistics (3 min)
- Occupancy: living there, vacant, tenant?
- Pets, showing restrictions, work-from-home hours, kids' nap times.
- Who holds keys. Any alarm codes.
- Mortgage payoff estimate (rough). Any liens, solar leases, HOA transfer fees.

Close (2 min)
"Here's what happens next. [VA NAME] sends you a short packet tomorrow. Read pages 2 and 3,
that's where our pricing conversation starts. At the meeting I'll walk the house, show you
what buyers are paying for homes like yours right now, and walk you through exactly how we
market it. If it makes sense to work together, we can sign that day. If not, no pressure,
you'll still leave with a plan. Sound fair?"
```

After the call: Charles posts a 3-line summary in `#listings`. VA finishes the Seller Intake Form, saves it to `04-Marketing-Kit`, and sets the temperature tag (`A`, `B`, `C`).

## 3. Pre-listing packet contents

Delivered 2 days before the consultation as a PDF (Command Designs or Canva, template `Pre-Listing Packet`), plus a printed copy at the consultation. Keep it to 10 to 12 pages.

| Page | Content | Source |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cover: "[ADDRESS]: Your Selling Plan", photo of the home from the street (VA takes or pulls from tax record) | Canva template |
| 2 | How pricing actually works in [MARKET]: the price/exposure curve, the first-14-days rule, what "priced right" means | Static, updated quarterly |
| 3 | Snapshot of your market: last 90 days in [NEIGHBORHOOD], active vs pending vs sold, days on market, list-to-sale ratio | VA pulls from MLS the day before |
| 4 | The Maximum Exposure Standard: what every listing gets (photos, drone, video, 3D, floor plan, property page, social, email) with example screenshots | Static |
| 5 | The 14-day launch timeline (from section 8 below) | Static |
| 6 | What happens after we're live: Monday updates, 24h feedback, open houses | Static |
| 7 | Contract to close: the milestones and how we manage them | Static |
| 8 | About Charles and the team, reviews, recent sales | Static, reviews refreshed monthly |
| 9 | Seller prep checklist (short version) | Static |
| 10 | Questions to ask any agent you interview | Static |
| 11 | Next steps and what to have ready for the meeting (mortgage statement, HOA docs, utility bills, upgrade receipts, keys) | Static |
| 12 | Contact page: [PHONE], [EMAIL], [CALENDLY LINK], charlesbrewer.guru | Static |

## 4. CMA prep checklist (VA pulls, Charles decides)

Due the day before the consultation. Output: a one-page pricing summary plus the MLS CMA report.

- [ ] Subject property details confirmed against tax record (sqft, lot, year, beds, baths)
- [ ] Sold comps: 3 to 6, last 90 days (extend to 180 if needed), within 0.5 mile (extend to 1 mile if needed), plus or minus 20 percent sqft, similar age and condition
- [ ] Pending comps: all within the same radius
- [ ] Active comps: everything the buyer for this home will also see, with days on market and price changes
- [ ] Expired and withdrawn in last 6 months, with their list prices (this is the "what did not work" list)
- [ ] Adjustments noted: sqft, lot, garage, pool, condition, upgrades, view
- [ ] Price per sqft range: low, median, high for solds
- [ ] Absorption rate: months of inventory in the price band
- [ ] List-to-sale ratio for the neighborhood, last 90 days
- [ ] Average days on market, last 90 days, and the split between under 14 days and over 30 days
- [ ] Seller's mortgage payoff and estimated net at three price points (VA builds net sheet with title company's fee estimate)
- [ ] Zillow Zestimate and Redfin estimate captured (to address, not to use)
- [ ] `CB Seller Concierge` run with the comps to draft the pricing talk track and the top 3 likely objections
- [ ] Everything saved to `05-Docs/CMA-[date]`

## 5. Listing consultation agenda (Charles, 60 to 90 minutes, MREA consultative style)

Bring: printed packet, CMA, net sheet at three prices, listing agreement ready in DocuSign, tablet with example listing media, the seller prep checklist.

| Time | Segment | What happens | Goal |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Connect | Sit at the kitchen table, not the living room. "Before we walk, tell me about the house from your side." | They talk, you listen |
| 0:10 | Walk the home | Seller leads. Take notes on the Intake Form. Ask about each upgrade. Note repairs, staging needs, photo angles | Learn the product, spot issues |
| 0:30 | Their needs | Restate what you heard on the intake call. "Did I get that right? What did I miss?" Confirm timeline, deadlines, decision makers | Agreement on what success means |
| 0:40 | The market | Page 3 of the packet. What's selling, what's sitting, why. Show the expired list | Ground pricing in evidence |
| 0:50 | Pricing conversation | Comps, adjustments, the range. Recommend a list price and explain the strategy. Net sheet at three prices. Talk track in section 6 | A price they understand and can defend to their brother-in-law |
| 1:05 | Marketing plan | The Maximum Exposure Standard, show real examples on the tablet: photos, video, 3D, property page, social sequence, email blast. The 14-day timeline. Monday updates and 24h feedback | They see the difference between listing and marketing |
| 1:15 | Agreement | "Based on what we've covered, are you comfortable moving forward with me?" Handle questions. Review the listing agreement, commission, term, price. Sign in DocuSign | Signed listing |
| 1:25 | Next steps | Set the launch date, photo date, vendor access. Explain what [VA NAME] will send tonight. Collect keys or arrange lockbox | Launch is on the calendar |

Objection handling notes:
- "We want to try a higher price first": Show the expired list and the first-14-days data. "We get one launch. The buyers who see it in week one are the ones who pay full price. Overpriced homes help sell the neighbor's house." Offer a written price-review checkpoint at Day 14 if they need it.
- "Another agent said they'd list it for more": "Any agent can list at any number. Ask them to show you the three comps that support it."
- "Will you cut your commission?": "The marketing you just saw is what the commission pays for. I'd rather earn you more at the closing table than save you a point on the way in."
- "We need to think about it": "Of course. What specifically would you want to think through? Let's do it together now while I'm here."

## 6. Pricing strategy talk track (Charles)

Use `CB Seller Concierge` to tailor this to the actual comps the night before.

```
"Here's how I think about price. Buyers in [NEIGHBORHOOD] right now are seeing [X] homes in
your range. They've already seen all of them online. When yours hits, they'll compare it to
those in about ten seconds.

Homes that sold in the last 90 days: [comp 1] at $[X], [comp 2] at $[X], [comp 3] at $[X].
Yours has [advantage] but [disadvantage] compared to those. That puts the fair range at
$[LOW] to $[HIGH].

The homes that expired: [expired 1] listed at $[X], sat [N] days, came off. Those sellers
tried the high number first. Now everyone who looked knows it.

My recommendation is $[PRICE]. That does three things: it lands in the search bracket where
[buyer type] is looking, it looks like value next to [active comp], and it gives us the best
shot at multiple offers in the first 14 days, which is when you have the most leverage.

If we launch there and get [N] showings in the first week with no offer, we know it's price
and we adjust fast. If we launch high and get no showings, we've lost the week that matters
most. Which of those would you rather deal with?"
```

Three price scenarios to show on the net sheet:
1. Recommended price (the one you defend)
2. Seller's expected price (show the DOM risk)
3. Aggressive price (show the multiple offer scenario)

Book everything the day the listing is signed. Confirm 48 hours before each. All vendors are in Command with the `Vendor` tag.
