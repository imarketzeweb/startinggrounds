# YouTube (parked until the systems below are running)

Do not start YouTube until these are true for 8 consecutive weeks (scorecard lines):
- 2 appointments per week
- 100% of listings meet the Maximum Exposure Standard on Day 0
- 5 social posts + 2 Reels published every week
- Monday seller reports out on time

Why wait: YouTube rewards consistency over years. Starting before the machine runs means it stops the first busy month.

## What to do now so YouTube is easy later

1. **Record raw material anyway.** The bi-weekly Tuesday content batch (4 face-to-camera videos) and every listing walkthrough are YouTube footage. Save the full-length originals in `Content/YouTube-Raw/[YYYY-MM]/` in Drive.
2. **Own the channel.** Create the channel now under the brand, set the banner, link charlesbrewer.guru, and upload every listing's 60–90s video unlisted (share the unlisted link in the seller report and on the property page).
3. **Collect questions.** The VA logs every question a client or lead asks (from calls, DMs, consults) in a "Questions" sheet. That list is the first 50 video titles.

## Launch plan (when the gate above is passed)

| Phase | Weeks | Output | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Foundation | 1–2 | Channel art, 3 playlists (Buying in [MARKET], Selling in [MARKET], Investing in [MARKET]), intro video | `CB Ops Manager` drafts channel description |
| Batch 1 | 3–4 | 8 videos recorded in 2 sessions (4 each) from the Questions sheet, 5–8 min each | Same Tuesday block, longer |
| Cadence | 5+ | 1 long video per week + 3 Shorts cut from it | VA edits in CapCut; Shorts also go to Reels/TikTok |
| Listing videos | ongoing | Every listing's walkthrough becomes a public video at Just Listed and moves to a "Sold" playlist at closing | Title format: `[Neighborhood] home for sale | [Beds]bd [Baths]ba | [Address]` |
| Tutorials | month 3+ | "How to sell a home in [MARKET]" series (6 parts), "First-time buyer in [MARKET]" series (6 parts), "Analyzing a rental in [MARKET]" series (4 parts) | Each series becomes a lead magnet on the site |

Every video description: link to the matching charlesbrewer.guru page with `utm_source=youtube`, the Calendly link, and the phone number. Every video ends with the same CTA: *"If you are thinking about buying, selling, or investing in [MARKET], the link below gets you 20 minutes with me."*

Hire trigger for a video editor: 2 long videos per week sustained for 8 weeks (see `01-MREA-MODELS/organizational-model.md`).
