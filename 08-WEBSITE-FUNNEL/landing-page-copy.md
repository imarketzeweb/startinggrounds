# Landing Page Copy: Home Hero, Sell, Buy, Invest

Read `/00-START-HERE/conventions.md` first.

How to use: the VA pastes this copy into the page builder section by section. Replace every placeholder. Charles approves before publish. Copy is written to be Fair Housing safe: it describes the service and the process, never who lives where or who a home is "right for." Keep it that way when editing.

Voice: direct, warm, no hype, no exclamation points. Short sentences. Charles talks like a professional who has done this many times and still cares how it goes for you.

Placeholders: `[MARKET]` `[BROKERAGE OFFICE]` `[PHONE]` `[EMAIL]` `[CALENDLY LINK]` `[VA NAME]` plus `[YEARS]` (years in the business), `[HOMES SOLD]`, `[AVG DOM]` (average days on market for Charles's listings), `[REVIEW COUNT]`.

## 4. Invest page (Capital Partner page)

Hero headline: Fund vetted, fully underwritten fix and flip deals in [MARKET].

Hero subhead: I find the deal, run the numbers, manage the rehab, and sell the finished home. You bring the capital. Every project is documented before you commit and reported every week until it sells.

Button: Apply to partner
Under button: You will get a Sample Deal Memo from a closed project and a call from me within a few minutes during business hours. Terms on every project are set by attorney-drafted agreements.

Section: How the Capital Partner Program works
Headline: Seven steps, the same every time
1. I source. Off-market, expired, estate, and distressed properties in [MARKET] that my buyers and sellers do not want but a flip can fix. I see them before they hit the open market.
2. I underwrite. Purchase price, rehab scope and budget, after-repair value from closed comps, holding costs, timeline, and exit plan. It goes into a written Deal Memo. If the numbers do not work, you never see the deal.
3. You review. The Deal Memo lands in your inbox. You ask questions, walk the property with me if you want, and decide.
4. We agree in writing. You choose the structure that fits you: buy the deal outright, joint venture, or private lender. An attorney drafts the agreement. Your own attorney and CPA review it. Nothing starts until it is signed.
5. I manage the project. Contractor bids, permits, scope, schedule, and budget. You get a written update every week with photos, spend to date, and what happens next.
6. I sell the finished home. Professional photography, drone, video, 3D tour, floor plan, a dedicated page, a five-stage social campaign, and an email to my database. The same maximum exposure standard every listing of mine gets.
7. We close out and look at the next one. Final accounting against the Deal Memo, lessons noted, next deal sourced.

Section: What you receive on every deal
Headline: The Deal Memo
Bullets:
- Property facts, photos, and condition notes from my walk-through
- Purchase price and how it compares to the maximum allowable offer
- Rehab scope, line-item budget, and contingency
- After-repair value with the closed comps behind it
- Holding costs, selling costs, and timeline by phase
- Projected total cost and projected sale range, with the assumptions written out
- Red flags found and what still needs verification
- The proposed structure and what each party does
Line: Every figure in a Deal Memo is an estimate. Actuals are reported weekly and reconciled at close.

Section: Three ways to partner
Headline: Choose the structure that fits you
- Buy the deal outright. You purchase the property and fund the rehab. I manage the project for a fee set in the agreement and list the finished home.
- Joint venture. We share the project under a written JV agreement that spells out capital, roles, decisions, and how proceeds are split.
- Private lender. You fund the purchase or rehab as a secured loan at terms you and your attorney set. I handle everything else.
Line: Which structure fits depends on your goals, your capital, and your advisors. We talk it through on the intro call. Terms are always set by attorney-drafted agreements.

Headline: You bring capital. I bring everything else.

Charles: sourcing, underwriting, the Deal Memo, contractor management, permits, weekly reporting, staging, listing, marketing, negotiation, closing.
Capital partner: reviews the Deal Memo, chooses the structure, funds per the agreement, receives weekly updates, approves scope changes above the threshold in the agreement.
